About

Billiards in Zlín

Stylish billiard bar in the heart of Zlín

Charlie's House has been part of Zlín nightlife since 1992. We combine billiards, cocktails and a relaxed evening atmosphere in one place — ideal for a night out with friends, a date, a celebration or a casual match.

You will find us at Potoky 372, Zlín. We offer 4 billiard tables for pool and carom, online reservations and a bar with cocktails, wines, spirits, beer and soft drinks.
